What's changing

The change

From 1 April 2027, small retail, hospitality and leisure businesses will get full relief on a property with a rateable value up to £18,000. Today the cut-off is £12,000.

Rateable value

The VOA's estimate of a property's yearly rent. It isn't the bill.

Who it's for

Shops, cafes, restaurants, pubs, hotels, salons and gyms whose business uses one property. Not banks, betting shops, estate agents or offices. Chains will usually not qualify.

England only

Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland set their own business rates, so they aren't included. Why

How the list is made

  1. Take every business property in England from the VOA rating list.
  2. Keep those with a rateable value above £12,000 and up to £18,000.
  3. Check what each one is recorded as, such as "cafe" or "hairdresser". Clear fits go on the likely list. Vague or mixed ones go on the possible list.
  4. Match each postcode exactly to a constituency. No guessing.

What it can't tell you

Who runs the business now, whether they have other premises, or what they pay today. Only the council can confirm eligibility. A business missing from the list may still qualify.
