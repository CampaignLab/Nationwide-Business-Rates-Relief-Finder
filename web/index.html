How we produce this list

We take the VOA rating-list assessment, select values above £12,000 and up to £18,000, screen the recorded premises use, match the postcode exactly to an English constituency, then flag uncertainties. Clear qualifying descriptions enter the likely-candidate list. Generic shops, conditional uses, possible exclusions and firm-name matches enter the review list. These are automatic screening outcomes, not a council decision.

VOA names and addresses are preserved. Separately supplied trading names show their source and remain unverified. They do not establish eligibility. A missing name does not exclude a property, and a matching name does not prove common occupation.

The change is announced for 1 April 2027. Rules and source dates appear above; sector mappings remain draft. We cannot establish occupation, other properties, existing reliefs or whether a bill newly becomes £0. Confirm with the billing council before making that statement. Absence from this list does not establish ineligibility.
