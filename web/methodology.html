What this list can tell you

This is a national automatic screening tool for English property assessments that may qualify for expanded full small business rates relief from 1 April 2027. It is not a list of confirmed recipients or businesses proven to move from a positive bill to £0.

Our process

  1. Start with the VOA assessment. We preserve the property reference, address, firm name, rateable value and premises description. The baseline is epoch 0004, dated 1 October 2026; the latest record date is 29 September 2026. Constituencies use the August 2026 ONS Postcode Directory.
  2. Select the value band. Above £12,000 and up to £18,000 inclusive. Properties at £12,000 are outside this new-full-relief target; above £18,000 is also outside it.
  3. Screen premises use. Specific qualifying descriptions without detected conflicts are likely candidates. Generic shops, mixed or conditional uses, possible excluded activities and uncertain mappings need review. Explicitly non-qualifying uses are outside target. Recreational marinas can qualify; an unspecified marina needs review.
  4. Locate the assessment. Only an exact postcode match assigns an English constituency. Unmatched records remain in a separate review download. The council comes from the VOA billing-authority code, never the constituency.
  5. Retain uncertainty. Firm-name matches flag possible multiple occupation, but do not establish a common ratepayer. Missing names do not establish sole-property occupation. All screening results require the actual occupier's circumstances to be checked.

Names and council links

The original VOA firm name remains available. Trading names from the independent Earley and Woodley spreadsheet are marked unverified and carry the workbook, sheet, row and recording date. They are display information and do not determine automatic eligibility. Names can be stale, and one property assessment is not necessarily one business.

Council homepages are sourced from the government's Planning Data local-authority directory, retrieved 3 November 2026, matched using the VOA authority code/address custodian identifier with explicit exceptions. Some legacy authorities have successor homepages. Use the site's business-rates section. A directory link is not council confirmation of any property.

Rules and limits

The October 2026 Budget announcement supplies the April 2027 thresholds. Our rules snapshot is 28 October 2026, marked announced and not yet in regulations. The official RHL guidance informs premises-use screening; actual use and the final rules govern entitlement.

VOA data does not establish occupation, the legal ratepayer, their other properties, existing reliefs, a council award, or the amount currently payable. A recorded premises type may differ from current use. Our “likely” label is not a measured probability. Automated checks and draft sector mappings can miss cases or include unsuitable ones.

Legacy eligibility and relief-percentage fields are retained in exports for comparison. They are modelled results under assumptions, not awards. The new screening outcome is separate: a potential multiple-property match goes to review even where the old model excluded it. We do not calculate actual savings or claim that a bill will newly become zero.

The independent spreadsheet comparison

The spreadsheet contains 64 distinct assessments. The previous live list contained 88 in Earley and Woodley: 56 overlap, eight are spreadsheet-only, and 32 are live-list-only, giving 96 distinct comparison assessments. Rateable values match the shared VOA snapshot.

Of the eight spreadsheet-only records, three are exactly £12,000, two match Reading Central, two are described as stores, and one is a golf-club site-office assessment with an unresolved postcode. This does not establish that either list is otherwise correct. The old live list includes generic shops with evidence of estate-agency use, which require review. The spreadsheet adds many useful names, but does not establish entitlement.

Before contacting a business

These properties appear likely to qualify for expanded full small business rates relief based on their rateable value and recorded premises use. The data does not confirm the current occupier, their other properties, existing reliefs or final bill. Before telling a business that it will have nothing to pay, confirm its circumstances with the linked billing council.

For an unverified case, say “this property may qualify; please check with your council”, rather than “you will have nothing to pay”. Reviewed names, use and postcode corrections should carry their evidence; do not silently replace source data.
