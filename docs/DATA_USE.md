# Data use and attribution

Provider information checked **3 November 2026**. These notes describe the sources and the project's publication; they do not grant new rights or claim provider endorsement.

## VOA rating-list data

The [VOA download page](https://voaratinglists.blob.core.windows.net/html/rlidata.htm) says its datasets use a restricted licence rather than the Open Government Licence. It says recipients of onward transfers must be told that its terms apply. Read the [current VOA terms](https://www.tax.service.gov.uk/business-rates-find/terms-and-conditions) for the permitted purposes and conditions.

The repository includes deliberately staged property-level outputs; the original downloads and intermediate files remain excluded. Publication here is not a blanket assurance that every downstream use, distribution or outreach purpose is permitted. No written VOA endorsement or clearance is claimed. Follow the source terms and assess the requirements relevant to your own use.

Attribution: Valuation Office rating-list data, Crown copyright and database right. The outputs retain provider notices and methodology disclaimers. An older repository decision log prescribed a blanket hold on sharing; the current documentation supersedes that project instruction without changing the provider's terms.

The August 2026 ONS Postcode Directory supplies postcode/constituency geography. Outputs retain: Contains OS data © Crown copyright and database right 2026. Contains Royal Mail data © Royal Mail copyright and database right 2026. Source: Office for National Statistics licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Consult the directory's supplied user guide and notices for the full attribution requirements.

## Council directory and supplied names

Council URLs come from the government's [Planning Data local-authority dataset](https://www.planning.data.gov.uk/dataset/local-authority), retrieved 3 November 2026. Each mapping record carries its source URL and date. These are directory-sourced official homepages, not verified relief awards or a guarantee that every endpoint is currently available.

The independent Earley and Woodley workbook is a comparison source. Its 61 non-empty supplied name records are preserved with workbook/sheet/row provenance and an unverified status. The full original workbook is not included. Public trading names may be stale or identify an owner, site or operator other than the legal ratepayer.

## Reusing and correcting results

Retain the source dates, provider notices, name provenance and screening disclaimer when sharing extracts. Do not describe the results as confirmed recipients or a definitive government/council mailing list. Report corrections through the [contribution process](../CONTRIBUTING.md), keeping confidential information out of public issues.
